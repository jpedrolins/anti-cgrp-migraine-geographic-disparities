## Proposed caption for Figure 2 v2

**Figure 2. Global distribution of country–trial participations in anti-CGRP monoclonal
antibody trials and representation relative to migraine burden.**
(**a**) Number of country–trial participations in each of the 194 WHO Member States
(453 participations in 47 Member States; 147 Member States had none). Each eligible
trial contributed one participation to each Member State listed as a recruitment
country, irrespective of the number of sites. (**b**) Observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio,
with expected participations proportional to each Member State's share of all-age,
both-sex migraine years lived with disability (YLDs) in 2023 (GBD 2023). O/E = 1
indicates proportional representation; classes are symmetric on the ratio scale
around 1 and include their lower bound (e.g. 1 to <2). Grey, Member States with no
participation (O/E = 0); white with outline, areas that are not WHO Member States and
were not analysed. Boundaries from Natural Earth (1:50m), Robinson projection, are
shown for visualization only. CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; WHO, World Health
Organization; YLD, year lived with disability.
