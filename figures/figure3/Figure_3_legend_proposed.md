## Proposed caption for Figure 3 v2

**Figure 3. Geographic representation of anti-CGRP monoclonal antibody trials relative
to global migraine burden.** Dark bars show each group's share of the 453 country–trial
participations. Each eligible trial contributed one participation to each WHO Member
State listed as a recruitment country, irrespective of the number of sites. Light bars
show each group's share of global migraine years lived with disability (YLDs; GBD 2023,
all ages, both sexes, 2023), which is the share expected if participation were
proportional to migraine burden. The observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio is the observed
number of participations divided by the expected number (total participations × the
group's share of YLDs). O/E = 1 indicates representation proportional to burden; values
above 1 indicate overrepresentation and values below 1 underrepresentation; groups
without participation have O/E = 0. (**a**) WHO regions. (**b**) World Bank income
groups (fixed FY2019 classification). All 194 WHO Member States form the denominator;
the Cook Islands and Niue, which have no World Bank income classification, no
participations and <0.001% of YLDs, are not shown in panel b. Percentages were
calculated before rounding. The comparison is descriptive and does not represent
patient-level access or recruitment. CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; GBD,
Global Burden of Disease Study; WHO, World Health Organization; YLD, year lived with
disability.
