# Figure 4 and Supplementary Figure S3 — proposed captions (not yet applied)

## Figure 4 (replaces the current four-panel caption)

**Figure 4. Socioeconomic gradients in migraine burden-adjusted anti-CGRP monoclonal
antibody trial participation.** Each point is a WHO Member State with available indicator
data: (**a**) Human Development Index (HDI), mean of available years 2011–2023, n = 190;
(**b**) GDP per capita, purchasing power parity (PPP; constant 2021 international dollars),
mean of available years 2011–2025, logarithmic scale, n = 185. The vertical axis shows the
observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio: the number of country–trial participations divided by
the number expected if participations were distributed in proportion to each country's
share of migraine years lived with disability (YLDs; GBD 2023, 2023). O/E = 1 (dashed line)
indicates representation proportional to burden. Positive O/E values are plotted on a
logarithmic scale; countries without participation (O/E = 0) are shown in the shaded strip
below the axis break, where vertical position is arbitrary and only reduces overlap. Colour
indicates world region; point area is proportional to expected participations. Lines show
the fitted means of separate negative binomial models of participations with log expected
participations as an offset, and shaded bands show their 95% confidence intervals (HC3
robust standard errors, Wald); fitted values below 0.035 are not drawn. Incidence rate
ratios (IRRs) are expressed per 0.1-unit HDI increase and per doubling of GDP per capita
(both P < 0.001). In each panel the labelled countries (ISO3 codes) are the three highest O/E
ratios, the three lowest positive O/E ratios and the three largest expected counts among
countries without participation. Associations are country-level and do not establish
causality.

## Supplementary Figure S3 (new)

**Supplementary Figure S3. Exploratory associations of health-system and research
indicators with migraine burden-adjusted trial participation.** (**a**) Age-standardized
Healthcare Access and Quality (HAQ) Index, 2019 (GBD 2019), n = 194; (**b**) gross domestic
expenditure on research and development (R&D) as a percentage of GDP, year closest to 2019
within 2015–2023, logarithmic scale, n = 127 (data unavailable for 67 Member States,
including Lebanon with one participation). Axes, symbols, zero-participation strip, models,
confidence bands and label rule are as in Figure 4. IRRs are expressed per 10-point HAQ
increase and per doubling of R&D expenditure (both P < 0.001). These models are exploratory
and were not mutually adjusted; sensitivity analyses using the GBD YLD uncertainty bounds
are reported in Supplementary Table S6.
