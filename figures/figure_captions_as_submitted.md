# Figure captions as submitted (24 November 2026)

Text of the captions of Figures 1–5 in the closed manuscript. The earlier `*_legend_proposed.md` files in each figure folder are drafts and are superseded by this file.


**Figure 1.** Flow diagram for identification and selection of eligible registry records. Records identified through ClinicalTrials.gov and WHO ICTRP were deduplicated or consolidated before full-record screening. The diagram reports the numbers screened, excluded, and included. WHO ICTRP indicates World Health Organization International Clinical Trials Registry Platform.


**Figure 2.** Global distribution of country–trial participations in anti-CGRP monoclonal antibody trials and representation relative to migraine burden. (a) Number of country–trial participations in each of the 194 WHO Member States (453 participations in 47 Member States; 147 Member States had zero participations in the primary analysis). Each eligible trial contributed one participation to each Member State listed as a recruitment country, irrespective of the number of sites. (b) Observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio, with expected participations proportional to each Member State’s share of all-age, both-sex migraine years lived with disability (YLDs) in 2023 (GBD 2023). O/E = 1 indicates proportional representation; classes are symmetric on the ratio scale around 1 and include their lower bound (e.g. 1 to <2). Gray, Member States with no eligible participation identified in the primary analysis (O/E = 0); white with outline, areas that are not WHO Member States and were not analyzed. Boundaries from Natural Earth (1:50m), Robinson projection, are shown for visualization only. Countries listed as removed from registry records were excluded from the primary analysis; their removal does not establish whether recruitment occurred before removal. CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; WHO, World Health Organization; YLDs, years lived with disability.


**Figure 3.** Geographic representation of anti-CGRP monoclonal antibody trials relative to global migraine burden. Dark bars show each group’s share of the 453 country–trial participations. Each eligible trial contributed one participation to each WHO Member State listed as a recruitment country, irrespective of the number of sites. Light bars show each group’s share of global migraine years lived with disability (YLDs; GBD 2023, all ages, both sexes), which is the share expected if participation were proportional to migraine burden. The observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio is the observed number of participations divided by the expected number (total participations × the group’s share of YLDs). O/E = 1 indicates representation proportional to burden; values above 1 indicate overrepresentation and values below 1 underrepresentation; groups with zero participations in the primary analysis have O/E = 0. (a) WHO regions. (b) World Bank income groups (fixed FY2019 classification). All 194 WHO Member States form the denominator; the Cook Islands and Niue, which have no World Bank income classification, no participations and <0.001% of YLDs, are not shown in panel b. Percentages were calculated before rounding. The comparison is descriptive and does not represent patient-level access or recruitment. CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; GBD, Global Burden of Disease Study; WHO, World Health Organization; YLDs, years lived with disability.


**Figure 4.** Socioeconomic gradients in migraine burden-adjusted anti-CGRP monoclonal antibody trial participation. Each point is a WHO Member State with available indicator data: (a) Human Development Index (HDI), mean of available years 2011–2023, n = 190; (b) GDP per capita, purchasing power parity (PPP; constant 2021 international dollars), mean of available years 2011–2025, logarithmic scale, n = 185; (c) age-standardized Healthcare Access and Quality (HAQ) Index, 2019, n = 194; (d) gross domestic expenditure on research and development (R&D) as a percentage of GDP, year closest to 2019 within 2015–2023, logarithmic scale, n = 127. HDI and GDP per capita were the primary predictors; the HAQ Index was a protocol-listed indicator evaluated in an additional analysis; R&D expenditure was added post hoc as an exploratory predictor. The vertical axis shows the observed-to-expected (O/E) ratio: the number of country–trial participations divided by the number expected if participations were distributed in proportion to each country’s share of migraine years lived with disability (YLDs; GBD 2023); O/E = 1 (dashed line) indicates representation proportional to burden. Positive O/E values are plotted on a logarithmic scale; countries with zero participations in the primary analysis (O/E = 0) are shown in the shaded strip below the axis break, where vertical position is arbitrary and only reduces overlap. Color indicates WHO region; point area is proportional to expected participations. Lines show the fitted means of separate negative binomial models of participations with log expected participations as an offset, and shaded bands show their 95% confidence intervals (HC3 robust standard errors, Wald); fitted values below 0.035 are not drawn. Incidence rate ratios (IRRs) are expressed per 0.1-unit HDI increase, per doubling of GDP per capita, per 10-point HAQ increase and per doubling of R&D expenditure (all P < 0.001). In each panel the labeled countries (ISO3 codes) are the five highest O/E ratios, the five lowest positive O/E ratios and the five largest expected counts among countries with zero participations in the primary analysis. Associations are country-level and do not establish causality.


**Figure 5.** Income-group distribution of anti-CGRP trials compared with WHO clinical trial benchmarks. Bars show the percentage of country–trial participations by World Bank income group (fixed FY2019 classification) for an exploratory anti-CGRP set and for three WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP) benchmarks: migraine trials, neuropsychiatric trials and all clinical trials (WHO Global Observatory on Health Research and Development). Each trial contributed one participation to each WHO Member State with at least one recruitment site; areas that are not Member States are excluded. The anti-CGRP set comprises all 132 registry records retained after deduplication and before eligibility assessment (108 eligible trials and 24 excluded records), so its total (477 participations) differs from the primary analysis (453); Supplementary Figure S3 shows the same comparison for all 167 records identified before deduplication and for the 108 eligible trials. n, number of participations; Difference, high-income share of the anti-CGRP set minus that of each benchmark, in percentage points. Low-income shares were 0, 0.05%, 0.34% and 0.61%, respectively; 12 neuropsychiatric and 45 all-trial participations without an income classification are included in the denominators. Registration periods, eligibility criteria, disease definitions and registry coverage differ between the datasets, so the comparison is descriptive. CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; ICTRP, International Clinical Trials Registry Platform; WHO, World Health Organization.
